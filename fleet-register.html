The Fleet Register

An annotated census of every wg deployment across the operator's four machines — 77 deployments cataloged fleet-wide, 76 listed here (the jobs organization is excluded), ~15,000 task records, January–September 2026. One cascaded. Generated from the public dataset (v1.1); content-free by construction. Pending grant applications are anonymized. The jobs organization is excluded per the privacy rule.

ulivo (the workstation) — 5 deployments

ulivo-wg-primary

The cascade — the book's whole subject. Thousands of tasks across eras (Jan–Jul ledger destroyed at the Jul 4 cutover; 1,192 measurable tasks preserved via quarantine + archive). Governance share accelerating without design; July multi-dispatch step 2.6%→25.8%→26.8%, never reverted. Recovered by a five-week subtraction arc; post-intervention burden small-n and unresolved.

ulivo-grant-a_R01 (anonymized pending grant)

848 tasks, May 19–Jun 5 — 78% governance share, flat by design; ~95% completion, 12-min median. The stable-machinery counterexample at scale.

Feb 13 baseline: 17 tasks, 0% governance, 100% completion — the tool's ground state before any accretion. Then Feb–May eras: 385 tasks, 0%→12%→24% governance rising linearly by design (FLIP per writing task), 93–100% completion.

lambda01 (the GPU server) — 12 deployments

lambda01-ndm

The largest stable-machinery campaign: 2,138 tasks over 2.5 months at a flat ~74–76% governance share. 94% completion. Average dispatch flat (~0.75–0.83) throughout; multi-dispatch rose only in its wind-down tail (16.7% at n=6). Shows machinery share at any level can stay healthy when bounded and designed.

Live research campaign on lambda01, Aug 1–present. Own corruption incident (Aug 1) with recovery dir. HEALTHY at snapshot — but the fleet's only rising burden signal (multi-dispatch 10.7%→30.8%, small n, live): the watch-list item, first candidate for continuous monitoring.
