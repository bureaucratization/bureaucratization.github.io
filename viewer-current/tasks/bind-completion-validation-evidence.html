Description

Make the ordinary completion review lane capable of reaching its Eval stage on real, correctly validated work. The recovery run showed every live FLIP rejecting because manifests contained only baseline git diff --check evidence even though workers had run the task's stated validation. Trace the evidence loss. Capture bounded deterministic validation command/result evidence during one-step wg done, bind it immutably to requirements/candidate/attempt, and expose it to FLIP without trusting worker prose. Keep deterministic validation authoritative and model review advisory by default. Do not lower reviewer standards, forge evidence, hard-code a Pass, or convert completion-review Eval into an Agency quality score. Add a controlled credential-free canary where valid evidence yields Flip Pass then Eval, plus missing/tampered evidence rejection. Ensure reviewer activity survives Done, daemon restart, and stale full saves under the latest daemon.
