Validation

  • Reject candidate A, revise to candidate B, and accept B through deterministic reviewer fixtures.
  • Exhaust the configured review budget in a second case with an already-settled Pi attempt and prove it becomes NeedsReview rather than Failed.
  • Dirty integration checkout produces LandingPending without changing user bytes; after the checkout becomes clean, one resume lands candidate B exactly once.

  • Restart between block and resume preserves the exact candidate and state.
  • Register the scenario in tests/smoke/manifest.toml with owner completion-resilience-e2e.
