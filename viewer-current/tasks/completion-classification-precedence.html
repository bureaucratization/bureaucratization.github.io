Description

Prevent a completed worker turn followed by a completion/finalization control-plane error from being mislabeled as a provider timeout or source execution failure.

Define and implement evidence precedence for spawn/wrapper terminal classification:

  • An exact terminal agent/turn receipt with stopReason=completed outranks weak timeout/reset text heuristics observed elsewhere in the stream.
  • Typed completion/finalization errors (NeedsReview, LandingPending, evidence/guard refusal) classify as FinalizationBlocked/Waiting and never source_execution_failed.
  • A genuine provider timeout before authoritative completion remains a provider infrastructure failure.
  • Conflicting or ambiguous terminal evidence fails closed without inventing semantic rejection, but preserves the candidate and names the ambiguity.
  • Projection must be deterministic and restart-stable; do not infer success from assistant prose alone.

Validation

  • Fixture from provider-backoff-contract: completed turn + later guard/finalizer refusal does not produce provider timeout.
  • Completed terminal receipt + incidental timeout text remains completed/finalization-blocked as appropriate.
  • Genuine pre-terminal transport timeout remains provider failure.
  • Conflicting exact terminal receipts produce a typed ambiguous state, not semantic/source failure.
  • Wrapper exit, raw stream classifier, lifecycle projection, and wg show agree.
